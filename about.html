Who We Are

A Multi-Category Merchant Exporter Built on Integrity & Modern Logistics

MY EXPORT WORLD was established with a singular mission: to serve as India's premier multi-commodity merchant exporter and trusted international trade house. By combining modern supply chain analytics with verified direct relationships across India's richest farming belts, certified manufacturing facilities, and industrial clusters, we deliver world-class commodities and manufactured goods to international buyers.

As an agile merchant exporter, we eliminate redundant intermediaries, perform meticulous factory audits, enforce international testing benchmarks (SGS, Bureau Veritas, Geo-Chem), and oversee the complete export workflow—from tailored OEM packaging and private labeling to DGFT customs documentation and seamless ocean container dispatch.
